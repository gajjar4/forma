# Roadmap

Where Forma is going. Nothing here has a date; what gets built next follows
what designers using it need most. If one of these is yours to build, say so
in an issue first, so two people don't build the same thing.

## Now

- **A dark theme.** The same board at night: the tokens are ready for it.
- **A download signed by Apple.** A Forma.app you can download in a browser,
  for people who don't use Terminal or an AI.

## Bringing research in

- **Meetings.** A call's decisions and the person's own words as a stack,
  each linked back to the meeting (Granola first).
- **Screens and flows.** Mobbin screens and flows as stacks, with where they
  came from.
- **Email.** A thread's attachments and the lines that matter.
- **A repo as a board.** Give your AI a GitHub link and it draws the project
  as a board: its files, its folders and how they connect.
- **Any tool through its AI.** FORMAT.md is the whole contract: anything
  that can write a folder can put research on a board. More recipes in
  SKILL.md as people find them.

## Finding things

- **Search by colour.** Every picture already knows its palette: find the
  references with that blue.
- **Across boards.** One search over every board on your Mac.

## Sharing

- **Send a board.** A board as a link a client can open and look around,
  without installing anything, with comments pinned where they belong.
- **Boards in a repo.** A board kept in git beside the code it informs,
  with changes you can read.

## Live cards

- **More dials.** Colours, curves and choices beside the sliders.
- **From a reference to a prototype.** Ask your AI to build a live card from
  the stack of references it should answer.

## Ideas, not plans yet

- Windows and Linux.
- A board on the iPad, for collecting on the go.
- Your AI's work showing while it's still working, drawn with the board's
  own dots.
