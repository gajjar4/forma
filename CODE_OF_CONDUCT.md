# Code of conduct

Forma is made by people with different skills: designers, developers, and
people who are only starting with either. Everyone is welcome here.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Say what you think of the work, never of
  the person.
- Explain your reasons. A clear "no, because" helps more than a silent one.
- No harassment, insults or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests,
  discussions or anywhere else the project lives.

If someone makes you feel unwelcome, message
[@davidpukhaa on X](https://x.com/davidpukhaa). It stays between us, and
behaviour that breaks these rules gets the person removed from the project.
